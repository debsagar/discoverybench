# Ambiguous before, separable after

Keep the world exactly as it is. Change only the question.

Do not ask "is this world hard to memorise". Ask:

> after a fair amount of watching, are there still several simple stories that
> explain everything, and is there one deliberate move that makes them disagree?

Yes to both means the world is worth keeping. No to the first means watching
already solved it. No to the second means nobody can solve it, which is a
different kind of useless.
