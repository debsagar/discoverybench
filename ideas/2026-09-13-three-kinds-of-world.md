# Three kinds of world

Every generated world falls into one of three buckets, and the bucket decides
what "solving it" even means.

1. **Compression worlds.** Everything you need is in what you see. A big enough
   lookup table wins. The only question is how much data it takes.
2. **Hidden-state worlds.** Something you cannot see is steering what you can.
   Watching long enough still tells you it, because the past leaks it.
3. **Experiment worlds.** Watching is not enough. Two stories fit everything
   that ever happened. Only doing something the world would never do by itself
   tells them apart.

We want the third kind. The first two are already benchmarks.
